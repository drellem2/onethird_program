==============================================================================
s5 — THE PRICE OF EACH REMEDY
==============================================================================
s5 — WHAT THE SEQUENCING RULE COSTS. Measured, not asserted.

The ticket forbids choosing a remedy before measuring the population, and it is equally
wrong to choose one without pricing it. Three candidates, three different prices:

  (a) LANDING DEPENDS ON ITS PARENT'S AUDIT. Price = the wall-clock the landing waits.
      Computed exactly: `audit_done - landing_claim`, per case, over the residue. A
      negative delay means the audit had already finished and the rule costs nothing.

  (b) LANDING MAY PROCEED BUT MUST RE-READ. Price = zero wall-clock, and instead a
      per-landing obligation to check. Priced here as the number of landings that would
      have had to perform the check, and the number where the check would have found
      something -- because a rule that fires on everything and finds nothing gets ignored.

  (c) FIGURE-PROVENANCE LINE PER NUMBER. Priced as the number of numeric literals the
      residue landings put into canonical documents, since that is how many provenance
      lines the rule demands.

  READ THAT TOTAL AGAINST THE ARC, NOT AGAINST A DAY: it is the ENTIRE cost of

  whole recorded history. The ticket's fear was that serialising would slow the arc.

(b) RE-READ — no wall-clock cost, a per-landing obligation instead

  count landings in the population              240
  count landings that would have run the check 44
  count where the check would have FIRED       30
  fire rate among those CHECKED                68.2%
  fire rate across ALL landings                12.5%
  BOTH RATES ARE THE HONEST PAIR. The first says: once you have an audited
  parent, a collision is the COMMON case, not the exception. The second says:
  the rule is silent on most landings, so it is cheap to carry.
  -> a check that fires on a minority is a check people keep. One that fires on
     everything is one they learn to dismiss, and that is the failure mode of (b).

(c) PROVENANCE PER FIGURE — how many lines the rule demands

  count distinct numeric literals the residue landings added to canonical docs 1739
  mean per landing 134
  -> (c) prices at roughly that many provenance lines FOR THE RESIDUE ALONE. Applied
     to every landing it is the most expensive of the three by a wide margin, and it
     is the only one that would have caught a figure copied from a parent that was
     never audited at all.

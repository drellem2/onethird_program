  mg-7522's own library states the property -- "an executable source
  that runs a shell script" -- and its `_TARGET` is `(\w+\.sh)`.

  ending in `.sh`.  Grouped by the target's BASENAME, because the
  basename is the thing the two-name rule tests.

==============================================================================
A3d  THE COMPARISON'S FIGURE -- `154 changed files`
==============================================================================

  The published document, section 3:

      "anchored to the pin the byte-comparison sees **154 changed
       files**; anchored to `HEAD` on a committed tree it sees 0, by
       construction."

  `s4_unpin.py` computes that with `git diff --name-only <ref> --`.
  Re-derived at every anchor that could have produced it:

      worktree vs the pin, now (what s4 prints)       275
      1ee1f1b vs the pin (the repair commit)          257
      1ee1f1b^ vs the pin (immediately before)        240
      HEAD vs the pin                                 275
      mg-7522's own committed transcript              166
      the published document says                     154

      *** NO ANCHOR REPRODUCES 154. ***

  THIS IS THE DEFECT OF SECTION 2 IN THE DOCUMENT OF SECTION 3.
  mg-05eb's OPEN 2 was a reader-facing artifact carrying a number
  its own instrument's transcript disagreed with.  Here the
  document says 154 and the instrument's transcript says 166.
  And the rule was already written down by mg-7522 itself, one
  commit later: "a number that moves belongs in a transcript".
  c252f96 applied it to the runner count and to the 2x2 totals in
  the SAME SECTION as this figure, and left this one as prose.

  WHAT IS NOT WRONG, so the finding is not read wider than it is:
  the CLAIM the figure supports -- that the pinned side is more
  informative than the HEAD side -- holds at every anchor above,
  and `s4_unpin.py` asserts the INEQUALITY rather than the number.

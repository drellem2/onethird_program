------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1  THE RULE, AND WHY IT IS DECIDABLE FROM SOURCE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  liveindex.py is cheap because a PATH-LIST-VALUED figure can be
  re-derived at an old commit by a short re-statement over
  `git ls-tree` output.  A CONTENT-VALUED figure cannot: re-deriving
  it means running the instrument at that commit, which is today's
  rule against an old tree.  So the question `is this transcript
  already checkable` is a question about WHAT ITS PRODUCER READS,
  and that is in the source.

      PATH-LIST-ONLY  reads a tracked path list and NOTHING ELSE.
                      A PROOF: the transcript is a function of the
                      path list, so the method applies for free.
      MIXED           reads a path list AND content/history/blame.
                      NOT a refutation -- consumers.py is MIXED and
                      three of its figures are path-list-valued
                      anyway.  It means A READER PER FIGURE IS
                      STILL OWED, which is the cost mg-ede8 paid
                      by hand for one transcript.

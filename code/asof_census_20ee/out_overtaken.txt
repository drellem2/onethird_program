6  WHAT THIS RULE CANNOT DO
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  1.  NOT OVERTAKEN PROVES NOTHING.  A transcript goes stale when
      its CORPUS moves and its producer does not, which is how
      out_consumers.txt shipped stale at its own commit in 5 of 8
      versions.  This rule is blind to every one of those.  N34.

  2.  OVERTAKEN IS NOT `WRONG`.  The producer's code moved; whether
      it reaches the printed figure is a question about the figure,
      and section 4 is a witness rather than an answer.

  3.  THE PAIRING IS A CONVENTION.  240 transcript(s) are outside it
      even after the widening, and they are counted rather than
      dropped -- a census that reports a number because it never
      looked is this directory's oldest named defect.

  4.  OVERTAKEN DOES NOT MEAN RE-TAKEABLE.  Section 5's first row is
      a transcript with a positive witness that CANNOT be re-taken
      at any revision, because the pin it recommended landed and its
      subject stopped drifting.  A rule that reported it as work to
      do would be sending its reader to a run that refuses.

  5.  THIS FILE IS OUTSIDE ITS OWN POPULATION, BY ARITHMETIC AND NOT
      BY RULE.  AS_OF is older than overtaken.py, so out_overtaken.txt
      is not tracked at the commit it reports on and cannot appear in
      the 883.  Graded by hand it is NOT OVERTAKEN and cannot become
      so: it is in run_all.sh, which is the whole difference between
      this page and the three in section 5.

  6.  ITS OWN WITNESS IS A WHOLE-OUTPUT MEMBERSHIP TEST, which is
      mg-9876 section 1's smell, found with a4_sweep.py's OWN
      detector over this file rather than by eye: `if v not in text`
      in grade().  IT IS KEPT, and the reason is not that it is
      harmless -- a literal that appears anywhere in the transcript
      for an unrelated reason suppresses a real witness.  There is no
      line-structured alternative that is correct, because a literal
      is printed INSIDE a longer line; the error direction is the one
      already declared above (the witness UNDER-states); and renaming
      the variable to miss the detector while keeping the semantics
      is exactly what a census that gates teaches its subjects to do.

S1a  THE VOCABULARY, AND THE 623 COUNTED BY RUNNING RATHER THAN BY ARITHMETIC
==========================================================================

  Every alternative of both vocabularies put through `_classify`, and
  then every unordered PAIR of them compared.  A1c prints the per-word
  table and then computes the pair counts as `C(n,2) - |SITE|*|EXEC|`.
  That closed form is correct only if no alternative lands outside its
  own list -- so here the pairs are compared one at a time and the
  partition is printed, which is the arm A1c does not have.

  the partition `_classify` induces on its OWN vocabulary:
      SITE        35 word(s)
      EXECUTION    8 word(s)
      BOTH         0 word(s)
      NONE         0 word(s)

  population: the 43 vocabulary WORDS of `_classify`, taken as unordered pairs
      ...vocabulary WORDS in SITE_WORDS                            35
          ^ one unit is one regex alternative;  label's own noun: `word`
      ...vocabulary WORDS in EXEC_WORDS                             8
          ^ one unit is one regex alternative;  label's own noun: `word`
      ...unordered PAIRS over those words                         903
          ^ one unit is one pair of words;  label's own noun: `word`
      ...PAIRS of those WORDS it tells apart                      280
          ^ one unit is one pair of words;  label's own noun: `apart`
      ...PAIRS of those WORDS it collapses                        623
          ^ one unit is one pair of words;  label's own noun: `collapse`

      the ticket's 35 / 8 / 903 / 280 / 623                REPRODUCED (a formality -- H1)

  AND THE PART THAT IS NOT A FORMALITY: only TWO of the four cells are
  occupied.  `BOTH` and `NONE` hold nothing.  A four-valued function is
  being used as a two-valued one, and that is a fact about the WORD
  LISTS, not about the arity.  S2 is the consequence.

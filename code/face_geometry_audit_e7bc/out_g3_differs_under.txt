S5 -- 'its expected value being taken from the predicate rather than from absorbable_bruteforce'
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the crux of the whole repair, and both `d2_deletion.py` and
`d4_auditor_rerun.py` say the change would put the two branches back
out of reach.  Made: `truth = absorbable_bruteforce(A, B)` becomes
`truth = tr.absorbable`.

   the substitution ALONE:                artifact BYTE-IDENTICAL, exit 0
   substitution + delete `shape`:         artifact CHANGED, exit 1
   substitution + delete `parity`:        artifact CHANGED, exit 1
  [HOLDS ] the substitution alone leaves the artifact BYTE-IDENTICAL and exits 0 -- on an unmutated predicate the two expected values agree, which is why nothing in the battery notices the difference until a gate goes
        WOULD DIFFER UNDER: the predicate disagreeing with the definition on one of the five constructed pairs, which is the state the row exists to detect
  [FINDING] THE NAMED CHANGE DOES NOT RESTORE THE DEFECT, and the statement says it would.  With the expected value taken from the predicate itself, deleting the `shape` gate still CHANGES the artifact (exit 1) and deleting the `parity` branch still does (exit 1).  Cause, measured: each row scores THREE things, not one -- agreement with brute force, agreement with the answer REGISTERED beside each pair before the run, and that the rejected pairs return at the named gate.  Killing the brute-force channel leaves the registered channel, and the registered answers are literals in `UNREACHED_GATE_PAIRS` that no mutation of the predicate can move.  The repair is therefore MORE robust than its own statement claims -- but the statement is still an untested assertion about a check, which is the defect one level up that this ticket names, and it appears in the transcript of `d4_auditor_rerun.py` as the sole differs-under of its headline 9-of-9 claim
   IS X THE FAILURE THE CHECK GUARDS AGAINST?  Yes in kind -- an expected value
   copied from the subject is the defect mg-d0e2 named -- but not in fact: here it
   is not sufficient to cause the failure the sentence attributes to it.

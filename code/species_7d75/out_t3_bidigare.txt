  Conventions A and B differ only by the order of composition in kS_n,
  so the claim is that B is IDENTICALLY the opposite algebra of A:

      c^U_{S,T}(Sol, B)  =  c^U_{T,S}(Sol, A)   for every S, T, U.

  If that holds, the column iso/B carries nothing anti/A does not, and
  likewise anti/B and iso/A.  Asserted before; computed here.

   n   pairs (S,T)   B(S,T) vs A(T,S)   CONTROL: B(S,T) vs A(S,T)

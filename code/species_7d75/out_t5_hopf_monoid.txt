T5e  CONTROLS -- four corruptions; two fire, two do not and why
==============================================================================

  (i) drop the lower-set condition from the coproduct.
      F  : prod closure 0  coprod closure 0  assoc 0  coassoc 0  compat 0
      control fires: NO -- see below

      THIS CONTROL DOES NOT FIRE, AND THAT IS CORRECT RATHER THAN A
      DEFECT, so it is recorded rather than replaced.  It was designed
      on the expectation that the lower-set condition is forced by the
      axioms.  It is not.  Aguiar-Mahajan Section 13.4.2 defines a
      ONE-PARAMETER FAMILY R_q of Hopf monoids of relations, with
      coproduct carrying a factor q^{e_{S,T}}, and records that
      'the Hopf monoid of posets of Section 13.1.1 is P_0', i.e. the
      lower-set condition is the q = 0 member.  The unrestricted
      coproduct is the q = 1 member, which is also a Hopf monoid.  So
      the corruption produced a DIFFERENT PUBLISHED HOPF MONOID, not a
      broken one, and what T5b/T5c establishes is closure, which holds
      for both.  Control (iii) below is the replacement that fires.

  (ii) use the TITS PRODUCT of faces in place of concatenation.  That
      is the product this repo's walk runs on; it is NOT the product of
      the Hopf monoid of set compositions, and it should break.
      F  : prod closure 1442  coprod closure 0  assoc 252  coassoc 0  compat 11020
      control fires: YES -- ON A TYPE MISMATCH, see below

      WHAT THE 1442 IS, AND IT IS NOT A NEAR MISS (mg-f8fa, on mg-a61f's
      X5).  Of 11301 pairs (x, y) tested, 1442 have BOTH ground sets
      non-empty, 1442 fail product closure, and the two sets are EQUAL
      rather than merely equinumerous: verified.

      mu_{S,T} takes its two factors on DISJOINT ground sets.  The
      Tits product intersects blocks.  Across disjoint non-empty sets
      every intersection is empty, so the Tits product returns the
      EMPTY composition on a non-empty ground set, which is not a face
      of it.  The control fires on a TYPE MISMATCH -- the two maps have
      different domains of definition -- and not because the Tits
      product nearly works.  The counts are weaker evidence than they
      look, and no count on either side could strengthen or weaken it.

      THE CONCLUSION DRAWN FROM THIS CONTROL IS NOT withdrawn, and it
      rests on the type statement rather than on the counts: THE BAND
      PRODUCT IS INVISIBLE TO THE HOPF STRUCTURE.  That holds at every
      ground set rather than on [4].  Nothing downstream that cites it
      -- mg-ebd8's and mg-af28's spectral work, lambda_2, Delta_AT --
      needs revising.  See code/species_remainder_f8fa/w2_typemismatch
      for the same measurement from disjoint code, with the control

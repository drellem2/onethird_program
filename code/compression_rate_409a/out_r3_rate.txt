r3.2  and the true alpha(A_n) is no larger -- Jacobi, measurement only
========================================================================================
    n |  measured alpha(A_n)  |  closed-form bound 6/(n(n+1))  |  1 - cos(pi/n)
    3 |          0.500000000  |                   0.500000000  |  0.500000000
  PASS    alpha(A_3) <= 6/(n(n+1))
    4 |          0.292893219  |                   0.300000000  |  0.292893219
  PASS    alpha(A_4) <= 6/(n(n+1))
    5 |          0.190983006  |                   0.200000000  |  0.190983006
  PASS    alpha(A_5) <= 6/(n(n+1))

  The third column is what Aldous' spectral gap theorem (Caputo-Liggett-Richthammer)
  predicts for this chain on the antichain: gap = (2-2cos(pi/n))/(n-1), so
  ((n-1)/2)*gap = 1-cos(pi/n).  IT IS READ FROM THE LITERATURE, NOT PROVED HERE, and
  no verdict in this directory depends on it; the closed-form bound above is self-
  contained and is what carries the Theta(n^-2) claim.

r3.3  is the antichain the minimiser?  min alpha over posets, per n
========================================================================================
    n |  population           |  min alpha  |  alpha(A_n)  |  posets within 1e-9 of min
    3 |  exhaustive           |   0.500000  |    0.500000  |  7 of 13
  PASS    n=3: the population minimum equals alpha(A_3)
    4 |  exhaustive           |   0.292893  |    0.292893  |  73 of 195
  PASS    n=4: the population minimum equals alpha(A_4)
    5 |  sampled(60,seed=909) |   0.190983  |    0.190983  |  34 of 54
  PASS    n=5: the population minimum equals alpha(A_5)

  THE ANTICHAIN IS NOT THE UNIQUE MINIMISER -- the value 6/(n(n+1)) is shared by a
  large tie class (at n=4, the argmin by raw float is the single-relation poset
  {(1,3)}, differing from A_4 in the 16th digit).  That makes the closed form more
  useful, not less: it is not a property of one exotic poset.
  n=5 is a SAMPLE, not a sweep, and the antichain is NOT in it -- the row says the
  sampled minimum coincides with alpha(A_5), not that nothing smaller exists.

r5.2  THE OTHER HALF, AND IT GOES THE NOTE'S WAY.  Does the FULL bias multiset determine alpha?
========================================================================================
  r5.1 refutes the SCALAR delta.  The note's phrase is looser than that, so the
  stronger reading is tested too: the whole multiset {min(p_xy, 1-p_xy)} -- an
  isomorphism invariant.  A collision with different alpha would refute that reading.
  VACUITY CONTROL: a bucket holding one isomorphism class proves nothing, so the
  number of buckets that genuinely MERGE distinct classes is reported beside it.

   n |  population            |  buckets |  merging >1 iso class |  buckets w/ alpha spread
    4 |  exhaustive            |       10 |                     4 |  0
  PASS    n=4: the test is NOT vacuous -- buckets do merge non-isomorphic posets   4 such buckets
    5 |  exhaustive            |       33 |                    22 |  0
  PASS    n=5: the test is NOT vacuous -- buckets do merge non-isomorphic posets   22 such buckets
    6 |  sampled(60,seed=4409) |       39 |                     5 |  0
  PASS    n=6: the test is NOT vacuous -- buckets do merge non-isomorphic posets   5 such buckets

  RESULT: 0 collisions anywhere.  On every population tested, the multiset of pair
  biases DETERMINES alpha.  THE NOTE'S SUGGESTION IS NOT REFUTED IN THIS FORM -- it
  is the one place in this ticket where the note's instinct is supported by data.
  It is an n <= 5 exhaustive + n = 6 sampled observation, not a theorem, and it says
  nothing about whether the determining function is USABLE: r2's bar is a statement
  about alpha's VALUE, and no representation of alpha can raise its value.

r5 VERDICT -- SPLIT, and the split is the finding
========================================================================================
  (a) THE SCALAR delta DOES NOT CONTROL alpha.  A_n and Z_n both have delta = 1/2 --
      maximal balance, every incomparable pair at exactly 1/2 -- and their alpha
      separate by Theta(n^2).  Since delta < 1/3 IS the (1/3)-(2/3) counterexample
      condition, the connection the note hoped for cannot run through it.  This is
      the direction the corpus already predicted: docs/audit-stage-process.md:211
      records that the delta obstruction is PROVABLY NOT A MIXING OBSTRUCTION.
  (b) THE FULL BIAS MULTISET IS NOT REFUTED, and on n <= 5 exhaustive it determines
      alpha outright, across buckets that merge non-isomorphic posets.  Reported
      because it goes against this ticket's own direction of travel.

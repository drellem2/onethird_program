r6 VERDICT -- what the literature settles, and what it cannot
========================================================================================
  SETTLES (and W3 was right that it is off-the-shelf):
    * the FORM.  alpha = 1 - cos theta_min, exactly, by Halmos 1969; Qian
      (arXiv:2201.12500) applies precisely this to two-component Gibbs samplers and,
      per its abstract, reduces the intractable questions to matrix algebra.
    * POSITIVITY.  arXiv:2304.02109 (AAP 2025) proves solidarity: if one scan order
      has a gap, all do, and polynomial scaling is preserved.  For us that is already
      free -- r1.1 gets alpha > 0 from connectivity of the fiber graph in one line.
  CANNOT SETTLE:
    * ANY VALUE ABOVE 1.  1 - cos theta <= 1 is an identity, so no theorem about
      principal angles, published or future, can put alpha over r2's bar of >= 2.
    * A CONSTANT.  Solidarity transfers positivity and polynomial scaling, not
      constants; and Wilson (arXiv:math/0102193 S7, quoted by W3) already priced the
      sweep decomposition at a factor of about two on the UPPER bound only.

  CAVEAT, STATED: the two arXiv items were read at ABSTRACT level only (WebFetch,
  2026-08-12).  Nothing above depends on their contents -- the ceiling is proved in
  r1 and the bar in r2, both on this machine.

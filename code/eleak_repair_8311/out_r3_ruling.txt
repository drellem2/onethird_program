R3.1  THE POSITIVE ARGUMENT. The corpus's identity (Op-Form :220-227, quoted in
      mg-76b2 Lemma 2.1) reads <1_A,(I-S_P)1_A> = E|A\sigma(A)|. That identity is
      what makes 'a bound on 1-rho' and 'a bound on Phi' the same statement, and it
      is the hinge the whole Cheeger conversion turns on. It is a statement about a
      MATRIX on one side and a COMBINATORIAL COUNT on the other, so it can decide
      between two candidate counts. Both are tested against it here.

      Derivation, for the record, so the machine check is checking something known:
        S_P is an average of permutation matrices M_sigma with (M_sigma)[x,y] = 1
        iff y = sigma(x). <1_A,(I-M_sigma)1_A> = |A| - |{x in A : sigma(x) in A}|
        = |A| - |A n sigma^{-1}(A)| = |A\sigma^{-1}(A)| = |A\sigma(A)| by R1.2.
        Averaging over L(P) gives the identity. The CONVENTION has no such
        representation: |A| - |A n set(p[:|A|])| is not a bilinear form in 1_A at
        all -- it reads |A| twice, once as a set and once as a LENGTH.

R3.4  THE RULING, stated explicitly before any code changes.

      THE DEFINITION IS CORRECT. lib2de0.E_leak IS DEFECTIVE AND IS REPAIRED.

      The ruling rests on three things, in order of weight, and NOT on the fact
      that the definition is the definition:

      1. THE CONVENTION IS NOT A CONDUCTANCE. Conductance is a property of a CUT.
         R1.3 measured |A\sig(A)| != |A^c\sig(A^c)| under the convention on 457132
         of 683656 (permutation, cut) pairs to n=7. lib2de0.py:17 calls Phi_P 'the
         same quantity, read as a CONDUCTANCE, minimised over ALL cuts A'. Under the
         convention 'the cut' does not determine the value, so minimising over cuts
         is not a well-posed operation in the sense the Cheeger argument needs.
         The definition satisfies the symmetry on all 683656 pairs.

      2. THE DEFINITION IS WHAT THE CORPUS'S MATRIX IDENTITY COMPUTES. R3.1, 0
         exceptions for the definition and a failure count for the convention, on a
         matrix built here from Pr[pos_sigma(x)=i] and on 406 posets.

      3. THE CONVENTION IS NOT LOAD-BEARING. R3.3: all three of mg-2de0's published
         Phi assertions hold under BOTH readings, so nothing mg-2de0 concluded
         requires the convention. R3.2 bounds the blast radius by PARSING: two
         files, and the prefix machinery is untouched.

      WHAT THE CONVENTION ACTUALLY IS, named so it is not mistaken for a variant
      reading: |A| - |A n set(p[:|A|])| measures how far A is from being an INITIAL
      SEGMENT under sigma. That is a real and meaningful quantity -- it is the
      natural generalisation of K_k in the direction of 'prefix-ness' rather than of
      'function application', which is why the slip is an easy one and why it agrees
      with K_k on prefixes. It is simply not Delta_1 and not Phi_P.

  Note what the two OEIS checks buy: A000112 alone would be satisfied by
  an enumerator that both over- and under-merges by the same amount, and
  a missed class is exactly where an unseen cycle would hide.  The orbit
  sum n!/|Aut| is a check on the LABELLED count and cannot be fooled the
  same way.  Both agree at every n.

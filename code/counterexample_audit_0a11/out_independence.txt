A2.2  the e = 9 family to n = 11, and how much of it is new
==============================================================================
  Targeted exhaustive enumeration of every poset with e(P) <= 9 up to
  n = 11.  That class is closed under deleting a MAXIMAL element, since
  e(P - x) <= e(P) for x maximal, so the enumeration stays exhaustive.
  It reproduces the repair's group sizes 7 / 13 / 20 at n = 6 / 7 / 8 and
  carries the measurement three sizes further than the repair reached.

  qmass is computed DIRECTLY (from all Bell(n) partitions) to n = 9;
  at n = 10, 11 every member is a cut extension (A2.3) so its qmass is
  inherited, which is the finding rather than an assumption.

A2.4  THE COUNT THAT MATTERS: distinct cores, per group and overall
==============================================================================
  Two members with the same core have the same (delta, qmass) by A2.1.
  So a group of N members with C distinct cores offers C, not N,
  opportunities for the hypothesis to fail.

  n |  N | k extremal | distinct cores | extremal cores | repair's p | core-level p

  READ.  The separation is REAL and it holds everywhere it was looked at,
  including three sizes beyond the repair's reach.  What is not real is the
  GROWTH of its p-value with n.  1/7 -> 1/286 -> 1/38760 is not evidence
  accumulating; it is the same 5 posets counted with more chain elements
  glued on.  The repair calls n = 8 'a pre-specified test in a NEW
  POPULATION' whose 'family of tests has SIZE 1'.  The test family does
  have size 1.  The population is not new: conditional on the n = 7 group
  -- which the repair itself names as a generating observation -- the
  n = 8 result had probability 1, not 1/38760.

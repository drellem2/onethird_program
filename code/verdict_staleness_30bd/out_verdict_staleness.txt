          tree no longer says?
==========================================================================================

  the question    : mg-20ee's census measures STALE ADDRESSES — `path:NNN` into files
                    the instrument does not own — and says, correctly of ITS OWN
                    population, that the verdicts are not affected.  A transcript can
                    drift in its VERDICTS with no address moving at all, and such a
                    transcript is invisible to an address classifier BY CONSTRUCTION.
                    This measures that second class.  IT REPAIRS NOTHING.
  sweep head      : 5bdd26bf4d5e2f4f84fc714b91071068f72e997a
  the corpus is   : `main` at f2117f1.  THAT is the sha to quote and the one above is not:
                    the sweep head is a BRANCH commit, the refinery rewrites it on
                    merge, and a record naming a commit that will not exist is
                    mg-daba's orphan pin in a different file.  The branch adds only
                    this directory's own sources, which carry no tracked transcripts
                    and are outside the population by the same test as everything else.
  method          : re-run each candidate suite in an isolated clone at HEAD, diff
                    every tracked out_*.txt against its committed copy, classify.
  per-suite limit : 900 s
  produced by     : python3 -B sweep.py  (hours; executes instrument code)
                    then python3 -B report.py, which is what run_all.sh runs

==========================================================================================
§2  THE TOKEN SET — THIS CHOICE IS THE INSTRUMENT, SO IT IS QUOTED IN FULL
==========================================================================================

  A line is a VERDICT LINE iff, after normalisation, it contains at least one of
  these as a WHOLE WORD.  Case-sensitive: this corpus writes graded outcomes in
  capitals and prose in sentence case, and the case rule is most of the selectivity.

      AGREES          BAD             BROKEN          CAUGHT          CLEAN           CONFIRMED
      DIFFER          DIFFERS         DISAGREES       FAIL            FAILED          FAILS
      FALSE           FINDING         FINDINGS        FIRED           FIRES           GREEN
      HELD            HIT             HOLDS           LAUNDERED       MISS            MISSED
      MISSING         NARROWER        NOISE           OVERSTATES      PASS            PASSED
      RED             REFUSED         REFUTED         REPRODUCES      SILENT          SLACK
      TRUE            UNDERSTATES     UNFALSIFIABLE   UNMEASURED      VERDICT         VERDICTS
      WIDER           WRONG

  44 tokens.  A transcript is VERDICT-STALE iff the SORTED MULTISET of its verdict
  lines differs between the committed copy and the regeneration — after mg-f771's
  N1/N2/N3 and after this instrument's A1 (a `file.ext:NNN` address) and A2 (a sha, an
  ISO date, a clock time).  Sorted, so a verdict that only MOVED DOWN THE PAGE is not
  a verdict that moved.

  WHAT THE CHOICE COSTS, BOTH WAYS:
    over  — a corpus-size count sharing a line with a token reads as a verdict move.
            That is why the bucket is split: TOKEN is the strong claim, NUMBER is not.
    under — a verdict stated in a word not on this list, or in lower case, is invisible.

§7  WHAT THIS DOES TO THE REPORTED NUMBERS

      54  presumed stale     mg-20ee's original, superseded

  150 DOES NOT SUPERSEDE 32 AND IT DOES NOT CONTAIN IT.  That is the misreading this
  section exists to stop: the two are counts of DIFFERENT POPULATIONS under different
  questions, and neither is a correction of the other.  32 remains the authoritative
  already-stale ADDRESS count and this report does not touch it.

  THE TWO NUMBERS ARE NOT NESTED AND MUST NOT BE ADDED WITHOUT SAYING SO.  mg-20ee's
  44 candidates are the transcripts that carry a foreign `path:NNN`; this population is
  the transcripts whose GRADED STATEMENTS moved.  A transcript can be in either, both,
  or neither — code/species_extent_audit_6cb9 is in the second and NOT in the first,
  which is why nobody had counted it.

  Anything quoting 32 as "the staleness in the corpus" is quoting one of two classes.

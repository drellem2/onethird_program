==========================================================================================

  no row's class has strengthened from `number` to `token`.

  WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT SEE, and the second half is the important one.  The
  record is FROZEN: it changes when somebody runs `sweep.py --only <dir> --pass2`

  IT CANNOT SEE A TRANSCRIPT THE RECORD DOES NOT COVER.  A branch adding a suite
  with a stale transcript is invisible here until somebody sweeps it, and no cheap
  check can be otherwise: the measurement is hours.  §5 item 1.

§3  HAS THE LIST GROWN?
==========================================================================================

  NO.  Every verdict-stale transcript in the record has a row in OWNERS.json, and
  no row's class has strengthened from `number` to `token`.

  WHAT THIS CAN AND CANNOT SEE, and the second half is the important one.  The
  record is FROZEN: it changes when somebody runs `sweep.py --only <dir> --pass2`
  and appends, which is exactly what README §8 item 3 asks the next worker to do to
  the 22 timed-out suites.  Re-measuring one of those will turn UNGRADED rows into
  clean or into stale, and the stale ones are NEW ENTRIES — that is the growth this
  arm exists to catch, and until this baseline existed nothing could have.
  IT CANNOT SEE A TRANSCRIPT THE RECORD DOES NOT COVER.  A branch adding a suite
  with a stale transcript is invisible here until somebody sweeps it, and no cheap
  check can be otherwise: the measurement is hours.  §5 item 1.

§6  WHAT THIS ARM CANNOT SEE — the enumeration's own blind spots, stated
==========================================================================================

  1  IT GRADES THE RECORD, NOT THE TREE.  `the list has not grown` here means `no
     new entry in sweep_30bd.jsonl`.  The corpus has moved since f2117f1 and this arm
     cannot say by how much, because saying so means re-running the sweep.  A
     coverage figure read off `git ls-files` was considered and REFUSED: it would
     put a live-tree number in a committed transcript, which is the defect 649b186
     removed from report.py two commits before this one, in this directory.

  2  THE 105 UNGRADED ROWS AND THE 22 TIMED-OUT SUITES ARE OUTSIDE THIS BASELINE
     ENTIRELY, and OWNERS.json says nothing about them.  They are not clean and not
     stale; they are unmeasured.  Re-measuring them is how this list grows.

  3  219 PRODUCERS THE RUNNER-BLIND PASS CANNOT PARSE ARE NOT IN THE POPULATION, so
     they cannot be in the baseline.  Nothing in this estate regenerates or compares
     them and no baseline can make that untrue.

  4  THE `<sha> <subject>` DETECTOR IS NARROWER THAN THE FAMILY IT NAMES.  It needs
     a masked sha at the start of the line, so a walk that prints `HEAD's subject :
     <subject>` or a bare commit subject is missed.  FOUND BY READING, NOT BY THE
     DETECTOR: 4 of the 8 rows this baseline calls HISTORY-WALK carry no NOTE in §4
     of out_verdict_staleness.txt.  Reported, not repaired — widening the detector
     moves report.py's annotations and that is a change to the corpus's record.

  5  AND THE ONE THAT OUTRANKS THEM.  A successor working only these 150 is working
     the list mg-30bd's instrument could see.  §6.6 of out_verdict_staleness.txt is
     unchanged and still owed: mg-6cb9 was found by a whole-run before/after diff,
     by accident, and not by any enumeration including this one.  What §1 above adds
     is WHERE to point that diff — the 178 suites ./build.sh does not run.

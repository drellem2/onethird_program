§3b  THE ONE THAT WAS CAUGHT IS THE REMEDY, AND IT COSTS NOTHING TO COPY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  R1 and R6 ARE THE SAME RECIPE on two documents — same token count, every token
  2 000 characters — and they come back opposite ways.  `ratchet.py` is GREEN;
  `c0_concept_discipline.py` REFUSES at exit 2 and blocks the merge.

  The difference is one design decision, and it is already written down in the arm
  that has it.  c0 locates its sections by ANCHOR PHRASES and says:

      "If a heading is reworded the anchor is not found and this arm REFUSES
       (exit 2) rather than passing -- a rename must be LOUD.  It cannot be
       silent-green, because a gate that quietly stops checking is worse than no
       gate: it is a gate people believe in."

  THAT IS A DEFAULT-DENY ON THE DOCUMENT'S SHAPE, and it is what turns a
  count-valued control into one that cannot be passed by a document that is no
  longer the document.  The ratchet has no such check: `measure()` computes
  `bytes`, `lines`, `max_line_chars` and `lines_over_2000` and `verdict()` reads
  ONLY `words`, so the four finer numbers are on the transcript and gate nothing.

  So the remedy this branch would propose is not new machinery: it is c0's rule at
  e331's site.  IT IS NOT MADE HERE — that is a rewrite of another directory's arm,
  its transcript and its PREDICTIONS, and a demonstration binding by the back door
  is not a demonstration (mg-585e).  It is exhibited, priced at one predicate, and
  handed to that arm's owner.

§5  WHAT THIS ARM CANNOT SEE — its own coarser unit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1  THE POPULATION IS FOUR ARMS, chosen because their subject is a document.  A
     gated arm that reads one of these files incidentally is invisible here.  That
     is the same proxy-over-a-coarser-unit this arm is about, in this arm.
  2  NO .git, so every git-valued decision is out of reach: twin_pin section 7
     grades itself REPORTED, NOT GRADED here, and gate.py refuses outright.  A
     witness that a git-valued section would catch would still read as a witness.
  3  `WRONG` IS THIS BRANCH'S READING.  §3's damage columns are numbers, but that
     an emptied SCOPE or an `mg-0000` pointer is a DEFECT is a judgement — the same
     one OWNERS.json makes about `cause`, and it declares that nothing checks it.
  4  A recipe that no arm catches may still be caught by a person reading the diff.
     Every witness here is loud in `git diff`.  What it is not is BLOCKED, and the
     difference is mg-be37's: a detector with no addressee and one that never fired
     are indistinguishable, and so are a reviewer who reads and one who does not.
  5  THIS TRANSCRIPT'S OWN HEADLINE IS A COUNT, which is §6's defect in §3.  `6 of
     7 pass` is unmoved by a recipe DELETED and a weaker one ADDED, exactly as
     `twin.mutations_total` is.  What stops it here is that the table above prints
     the SET — every recipe by id, headline and verdict — so the count is a summary
     of something a reader can re-read rather than the record itself.  That is the
     only remedy this directory found for a count, and it is not a check.
  6  A MUST-FIRE PAIR PROVES THE ARM RUNS, NOT THAT IT IS SENSITIVE TO THE WITNESS'S
     KIND OF CHANGE.  Each pair moves a check of the same arm; none of them is
     evidence that the arm could in principle have seen its partner.  It closes
     `the sandbox is not exercising this arm` and nothing wider.

§6  THE ONE THE ESTATE ALREADY NAMED, READ AND NOT WITNESSED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  code/control_gate_724a/BASELINE.json gates two INTEGERS over the twin's negative
  control — `twin.mutations_caught` and `twin.mutations_total` — and the second
  exists because of this exact shape.  Its own `why`, quoted:

      "Gated because a mutation quietly deleted is coverage quietly removed, and
       it would otherwise be invisible: 16 of 16 caught reads exactly like 17 of 17."

  THE REMEDY IS THE SAME KIND OF ARTIFACT AS THE DEFECT: a count was guarded by
  adding a second count, so a mutation DELETED and a different one ADDED moves
  neither field.  That is mg-cda7's `gains lines, OUT unmoved` letter for letter,
  on the file that gates the gate.

  IT IS READ AND NOT WITNESSED, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS THE WHOLE POINT OF THIS
  DIRECTORY, so it is labelled rather than dressed up as a result: exercising it
  needs gate.py, gate.py needs the twin suite's git-valued sections, and this
  sandbox has no git.  It is the sharpest instance in the estate and it is the one
  this arm could not build.  Filed forward rather than claimed.

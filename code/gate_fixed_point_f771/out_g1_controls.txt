§4  WHAT IS NOT COVERED HERE

  nothing about whether the watched class is the right class.  A gate suite that
  writes a tracked file NOT named out_*.txt would be invisible to g0 and to every
  world above, and nothing in this directory would notice.  Named rather than
  discovered: no such suite exists today, and that is a fact about today.

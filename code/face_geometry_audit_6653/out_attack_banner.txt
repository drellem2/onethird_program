BOTTOM LINE. The literal is gone from the artifact and ATTACK A shows the new control
is a control and not a tautology -- both of mg-f2e1's E5 claims hold, and ATTACK E
shows the A4 repair underneath it is sound: unguarding the tally makes the SELF-TEST's
own assertion fail and the run exit 1, which is the right instrument catching it.

But ATTACKS B and C construct the F5 defect in full with the new control in place:
a [PASS]-prefixed line near the top of controls_output.txt carrying 'ALL CONTROLS
PASS', above a bottom line denying it, with the control reporting 'offending rows:
none' and exit 0. B is the cheaper route -- `detail` prints on the SAME LINE as the
row name, behind the same [PASS] prefix, so a grep cannot tell them apart.

The control scans ROW_NAMES only. Its docstring discloses that, and then says it
'makes the artifact's occurrences of the banner exactly the bottom line's, and claims
nothing further' -- the wider of the two adjacent sentences, and the one to strike.
ATTACK D fixes the other boundary: what the control buys is exactly 'no row name
contains these 17 characters', which is the right scope for it to have and less than
the docstring's last clause asserts.

B2  WHAT IS g1'S NEW COMPARISON INVARIANT UNDER?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
   g1 now decides "did the measuring half move" by diffing c1's printed
   sections (i)+(ii).  So it is blind to a measurement change that prints
   nothing.  THE REASON THAT CANNOT BITE, and it is a fact about c1 rather than
   an assurance: every quantity c1 COMPARES in section (iii) is drawn from the
   two dicts it PRINTS in full in (i)+(ii) -- mine_vertices and mine_edges.
   Printed is therefore a superset of compared, and a measurement change
   invisible to (i)+(ii) cannot move any of the 198 cells either.

   Checked two ways: by reading c1's source for what section (iii) reads, and
   by counting.

   'mine_' names the comparing half BINDS for itself     : mine_c, mine_named, mine_v
   'mine_' names it inherits from the measuring half     : mine_edges, mine_vertices
   'mine_' names the measuring half PRINTS               : mine_edges, mine_vertices

   c1's measuring half, as printed : 125 lines
   cells c1 compares in (iii)      : 198

   RESIDUAL, STATED: a change to c1 that alters neither its printed
   measurement nor its comparison is invisible to g1.  That is correct
   and not a gap -- such a change moves no cell of the reproduction,
   which is the property g1 certifies.

       [finding] THE REASON, and it is the note's own risk.  E_l annihilates everything
                 COARSER than l (a5.2) but READS everything FINER: a scale-l edge holds

                 and the note's step from (8) to 'a multiscale family of median-graph
                 problems' is exactly that step.
       [where it DOES hold] at the two posets whose finest scale carries no choice, the
                 identity is true for a degenerate reason and not a structural one.

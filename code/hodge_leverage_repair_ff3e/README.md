## The question this is organised around

Not *"how do I catch a label exchange"*. The assignment forbids that answer, and it is right to:
this is the **fifth generation** of one shape on this artifact and the **second** where a fix moved
the defect to the adjacent field.

| repair | what the census compared | what the next audit exchanged |
|---|---|---|
| mg-8916 | the **multiset** of figure tokens | mg-8aae exchanged two **figures** — a multiset is invariant under a transposition |
| mg-8eca | the **sequence** of figure tokens | mg-9207 exchanged the two **labels** — a sequence of figures is invariant under permuting what they hang on |
| **mg-ff3e** | **the whole record** | — |

**What generates the instances** is that the gate compares a **projection** of the section against
an expectation, and each repair enlarged the projection **by one named field**. The complement was
always *everything else*, so the next exchange simply moved into it. Naming labels buys one more
generation.

**The only projection with an empty kernel is the identity.** So the section is cut in two along a
seam **the record itself defines** — `partition` walks the figure-shaped tokens and returns
`(segments, figures)` — and both halves are compared, both positionally:

- **figures** → `ORDER`, by value and by slot (unchanged, mg-8916 + mg-8eca);
- **segments** → the declared record in `site_records.txt`, byte for byte, in order (new).

**No field is named, so none can be left behind because nobody named it.** Labels, table row
headings, column alignment, the text inside a marked quotation, and any field a later editor
invents are in `segments` the moment they are written, without `verify_landing.py` being touched.

## And "the two halves are the whole record" is measured, not said

`rejoin(segments, figures) == raw` is a **gate row**, per site, every run — the `RECORD PARTITION`
row. Without it, "lossless" would be a sentence beside the code, which is the exact shape both of
the last two findings had. **D3** makes the partition lossy (the segments compared
case-insensitively — a projection, and the shape of every repair before this one) and shows that
row going red at 3 of 3 sites: **a claim of exhaustiveness that cannot fail certifies nothing.**

## The cost, and why it is text and not a hash

Any edit to these three sections that is not a live measurement makes the run **red** until the
declared record is regenerated with `python3 verify_landing.py --reseal`. That is the fail-closed
cost and it is the same cost the roster already carries.

The declaration is the **skeleton text**, not a `sha256` of it, for one reason: a reseal has to be
**reviewable**. A hash bump is a rubber stamp with no content to read; a text diff is a thing a
reviewer can check. And `--reseal` **refuses** while any other gate row is refuted, so a section
whose figures are wrong cannot be blessed by resealing it.

**It is a declaration and not a duplicate**, which this arc distinguishes for a reason (mg-a318
F-1). A duplicate is a second place a *reader* meets the claim with nothing comparing the two, so
one goes stale in silence. The declared record is read by the gate alone and compared to the
document on every run: a divergence **is** the red run.

## This deliverable checked for its own defect

It is a comparison, repairing a comparison, and it compares things. R5 enumerates **six**
comparisons in it and says of each whether it can exhibit *"compared by identity on some fields
and position on others, so whatever is in neither is silent"*:

1. `SITE RECORD` — **can**, does not: `==` on whole strings, kernel empty by construction.
2. `RECORD PARTITION` — **cannot**: equality on two whole strings, no field structure. Its risk is
   the opposite one, unfalsifiability, and D3 answers that.
3. R1's scoring — **DID**, twice. `"FIGURE CENSUS" in row` matched the `SITE RECORD` row's own
   explanation, which names the other rows; and mg-9207's finding **IDs** were matched by prose, so
   `J-3`'s text (*"and E2 below…"*) counted as an E2 finding still standing. Both fixed by keying
   on the **heading** / the **ID** instead of the line. Both kept in `PREDICTIONS.md`.
4. R3's population — **cannot** in that sense: derived, nothing named. Its real limit is that it is
   in memory, declared in its own heading.
5. R4's re-run scoring — **can**. Narrowed: rows are counted from their markers rather than parsed
   out of a bottom-line sentence whose wording differs between the four instruments, and findings
   are identified by ID. Stated rather than dressed up.
6. The frozen-transcript check — **cannot**: a hash over a whole file has no field structure.

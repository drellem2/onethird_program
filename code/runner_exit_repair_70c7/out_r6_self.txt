R6a  E1 -- IS EVERY COUNT'S GRAIN STATED?
==============================================================================

  The defect is a count of SOURCE LINES presented as a count of RUNS.
  A rule that could decide this in general would be a rule about
  meaning, so the question asked here is the checkable half: every
  transcript line that reports a count over source carries one of the
  grain words, in the same line or the one above it.

      transcript lines reporting a count over source    22
      ...quoting another artifact's line, excluded       6
      ...with no grain word in the window                0

  EXTENT OF THIS CHECK, stated rather than implied: it establishes
  that the WORD is present, not that it is the right one.  A count
  labelled `executions` that is really sites would pass.  What it
  prevents is the shape mg-dee4 found -- a number with no grain
  attached at all, which cannot be argued with because it does not
  say what it counts.

      figures quoted inside a correction or a prediction   3

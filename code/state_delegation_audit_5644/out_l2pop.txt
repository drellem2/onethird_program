VERDICT ON THE NEGATIVE, WITH ITS BOUND
==========================================================================================
  'closing that by counting blockquotes would catch mg-218d's mutation and not
   the layer' — NOT TRUE AS STATED of a population rule.  The construction above
  is not a count and is not shaped around S1; it fires on every mutation that
  changes the blockquote population of the README, in both directions.

  'What would close L2 is a rule that decides which blocks ought to be certified,
   and there is not one here' — the rule above IS one, and its discipline is
  already in delta_control.py: section 8 is default-deny over an enumerated
  population, and this is the same move applied to the region set.

  THE BOUND, and it is why this audit reports the negative as OVERSTATED rather
  than FALSE, and files no BROKEN on it.  The construction closes L2 for
  BLOCKQUOTE blocks in ONE file.  A contradicting near-copy written as a plain
  paragraph, or as a table, or placed in STATE.md, is outside its population and
  still exits 0.  So mg-bee1's substantive point — that no rule here decides
  which blocks OUGHT to be certified in general — stands.  What does not stand is
  the specific claim that the available fix is mutation-shaped: it is not, it is
  cheap, and declining it costs more than the argument for declining it says.

t1  THE TRICK, REPAIRED so it needs no divisibility side condition
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    SHARP (b0 b3):   every ordering has a good run of 3   iff   g > ceil(2N/3).
    DANIEL's form:   p_xy > 2/3, i.e. g > 2N/3 -- enough iff 3 | N (b0 b4).
    REPAIR:          p_xy > 2/3 + 2/N   is sufficient at EVERY N and every residue.
      derivation: g > 2N/3 + 2 forces g >= floor(2N/3) + 3 >= ceil(2N/3) + 1, since
      ceil(2N/3) <= floor(2N/3) + 1.

    checked at every (N, g) with 3 <= N < 200: 0 failures   [PASS]
    AND IT IS NOT VACUOUS AT SMALL N: at N = 4 the repair demands p > 7/6 > 1, i.e. it
    is UNSATISFIABLE there -- correctly, because N = 4, g = 3 IS the counterexample.
    The trick simply has no content at N = 4, and the repair says so instead of
    quietly failing.

t2  DOES CRAFTING THE ORDERING CHANGE THE GUARANTEE?  Bruhat vs adversary
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    population: isomorphism classes n = 2..6 with |L(P)| <= 200 and a well-defined e.
      108 posets (4 skipped for |L(P)|, 287 with no e), 647 incomparable edges.

    edges with a good run of 3 in the BRUHAT-REFINING ordering:  608 of 647
    edges with a good run of 3 in the ADVERSARIAL ordering:      234 of 647
    edges meeting the criterion  g > ceil(2N/3):                 234 of 647
    adversary disagrees with the criterion at 0 edges   [PASS -- the criterion IS the adversarial answer]

    ⚠️  SO CRAFTING BUYS NOTHING FOR THE GUARANTEE.  The criterion is UNIVERSAL over
    orderings, so a Bruhat-refining ordering is guaranteed a good run of 3 on exactly
    the edges an adversary is -- and it HAPPENS to get one on 374 further edges, which
    is luck at that ordering and not a guarantee.  `Perhaps it's an extension of the
    weak bruhat order` therefore does not strengthen the trick; what it can do is make
    the triple MEAN something to whatever consumes it.

t3  WHAT THE BRUHAT STRUCTURE DOES BUY, measured
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    the ordering's BOTTOM element is `e` itself at 108 of 108 posets   -- so the run at
    the very bottom is good for EVERY incomparable edge at once, for free, and the
    content of the trick lies entirely in the runs further up.
    across 1820 runs of three consecutive extensions, the largest inv_e spread within a
    run is 1 -- so three consecutive extensions of a Bruhat-refining ordering are
    close in inversion count, which is the currency STATE.md:29 says a proof would be
    delivered in.  THAT is the reason to keep the trick in mind, and it is a reason
    about the CONSUMER, not about the guarantee.

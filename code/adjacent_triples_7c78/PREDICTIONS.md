## The four readings being tested

`σ` a linear extension, `e` the distinguished order the `> 2/3` majorities cohere into,
*adjacent triple* = the three elements at consecutive positions `(k, k+1, k+2)`, *e-aligned* =
those three in the same relative order as in `e`, *free* triple = pairwise incomparable in `P`
(equivalently: all six orders of that block are linear extensions).

| reading | statement |
|---|---|
| **R1** | for each incomparable edge `{x,y}` there is **some** `σ` in which `x` and `y` sit inside one adjacent triple, in their `e`-order |
| **R2** | there is a **single** `σ` such that **every** incomparable edge sits inside some adjacent triple of it |
| **R3** | in **every** `σ`, every incomparable edge sits inside an `e`-aligned adjacent triple |
| **R4** | bias away from `1/2` on `{x,y}` **forces a third element** distinguishing `x` from `y`; frozen makes that hold at every edge at once |

## Named conditions under which I would report `daniel-fact-is-a-new-lever`

Filed in advance so the verdict cannot be assembled afterwards.

1. A triple-level statement that is **not** derivable from `mg-92e6`'s involution — i.e. one
   whose proof needs a symmetry of `L(P)` that adjacent transpositions of incomparable pairs do
   not generate.
2. Any poset and pairwise-incomparable triple where the 6 consecutive orders are **not**
   equinumerous — which would refute P6 and mean the `S₃` symmetry is not free.
3. A bound of the form `Pr[configuration] ≥ c > 0` forced by `δ(P) ≤ 1/3` — i.e. the `"yields"`
   direction with the hypothesis doing work. My expectation is the opposite sign (frozen makes
   reversible configurations **rare**, not common), and finding a production statement rather
   than a cap would be the finding.
4. Any joint constraint on **three or more** pair biases that is not a consequence of the
   per-pair F1 plus disjointness of events.

## Errors I expect to be able to make here

- **E1 — confusing R1 with R4.** They differ in what is quantified and in whether the `> 2/3`
  hypothesis is used at all. Logging the true-but-hypothesis-free one as though the hypothesis
  earned it is the single most likely way this ticket goes wrong, and it is exactly the shape
  `FACTS.md` F1's `NOT` field guards against for the bias⟹adjacency direction.
- **E2 — measuring "0 failures over frozen posets" and reporting it as a clean sweep.** The
  population is empty. `a5` prints the count and the reason together, on purpose.
- **E3 — `e` is not canonical off the frozen class.** `e` is only forced when every incomparable
  pair is `> 2/3`-decided; at a balanced poset there is no distinguished order and any arm that
  silently picks one (say, the first linear extension) is measuring a **choice**. Every arm that
  needs `e` must either restrict to the boundary class or quantify over all reference orders.
- **E4 — "free triple" is not "three consecutive incomparable-in-pairs".** They coincide *for a
  consecutive block*, and that coincidence is a small lemma I should check rather than assume.
  Planted in `a0`.
- **E5 — iso classes vs labeled posets.** Every quantity here is an isomorphism invariant, so
  classes are the right population — but a broken canonical form silently merges classes and
  shrinks the population while the failure count stays `0`. `a0` checks the class counts against
  the known sequence `1, 2, 5, 16, 63, 318, 2045` (OEIS A000112) before any arm trusts them.

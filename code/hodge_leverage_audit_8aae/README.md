## Why it is not a re-run of mg-8916's own R1–R4

A repair scored only by its author's instrument is scored by the author. So:

- the **prose probe** uses this instrument's own sentence, in slots chosen here **by a procedure**
  and **controlled green before use** (blanked length-preservingly, the runner must stay green),
  with any slot inside a marked quotation rejected — a probe there would measure the declared
  exemption rather than the gate;
- the **census totals** are re-counted by a **second tokenizer that shares no regex** with the one
  that produced them (it took two corrections to get right, and both are recorded);
- **mg-835f's own instrument** is re-run here rather than quoted from mg-8916's transcript, and
  its committed transcript is sha256-checked untouched afterwards;
- mg-835f's **12 of 12** is re-taken at **row granularity** — the requirement is not that the run
  goes red but that the `READ AT THE SITE` row **for that figure** is the row that failed.

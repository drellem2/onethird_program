T8  Brown 2000 §4.3's hypothesis is a finite DISTRIBUTIVE lattice.
    Which standard branching graphs meet it?
==============================================================================

  Tested on INTERVALS [0-hat, x], not on truncations: a truncation of
  an infinite lattice at rank r is not a lattice at all (two elements
  of top rank lose their join), so it would answer the wrong question.

  Young's lattice: every interval [0, lambda] with |lambda| <= 6
      30 intervals, non-distributive: 0
  Young-Fibonacci: every interval [0, w] with rank(w) <= 6
      33 intervals, not a lattice: 0, non-distributive: 5
      smallest witness: w = (2, 2, 1), failing triple (a,b,c) = ((1, 1, 1), (1, 2), (1, 2, 1))

  Reading: of the two 1-differential LATTICES (Stanley 1988 for
  Young's; Byrnes 2012 that there are no others), only Young's is
  distributive -- the Young-Fibonacci lattice is modular but not
  distributive.  So Brown §4.3's construction reaches the Young graph
  and does not reach the other one.

T3c  THE CARTAN MATRIX --- the invariant that separates the two sides
==========================================================================
Margolis-Saliola-Steinberg (arXiv:1508.05446) Thm 4.18 computes the
Cartan matrix of the algebra of a left regular band and states that it
is 'unipotent lower triangular with respect to any linear extension of
the partial order on Lambda(B)'.  It is rebuilt here from their own
proof --- the character of kL_Y is chi(b) = |bB intersect L_Y|, and
chi = sum_Z C_{Z,Y} chi_Z --- and then tested for the three properties
that matter.

The self-check that makes this trustworthy: for a split basic algebra
(all simples one-dimensional) the entries of the Cartan matrix must sum
to dim A.

P               |Lam|    dim A    sum C nonneg int  unit diag   triangular  symmetric
antichain 2         2        3        3        yes        yes          yes         NO
chain 2             2        2        2        yes        yes          yes        yes
antichain 3         5       13       13        yes        yes          yes         NO
chain 3             4        4        4        yes        yes          yes        yes
V-poset 3           5        6        6        yes        yes          yes         NO
antichain 4        15       75       75        yes        yes          yes         NO
chain 4             8        8        8        yes        yes          yes        yes
antichain 5        52      541      541        yes        yes          yes         NO
chain 5            16       16       16        yes        yes          yes        yes

  sum C = dim A on every row, 0 exceptions: the computation is right.
  Unipotent lower triangular on every row: MSS Thm 4.18 reproduced
  against our object.
  SYMMETRIC exactly on the rows where the algebra is semisimple.

  Why that last column decides the question.  A cellular algebra in the
  sense of Graham-Lehrer --- Temperley-Lieb, Brauer, partition, Hecke,
  and the whole non-semisimple continuation of the branching axis ---
  has a SYMMETRIC Cartan matrix.  A left regular band algebra has a
  UNITRIANGULAR one.  A matrix that is both is the identity, and an
  algebra with identity Cartan matrix and one-dimensional simples is
  semisimple.  So the two families meet only at their semisimple point.

==========================================================================
T3e  MULTIPLICITY-FREENESS ON OUR SIDE --- an ARGUMENT, not evidence
==========================================================================
Every simple kF(P)-module is one-dimensional.  A one-dimensional module
over any subalgebra is simple, so along ANY chain of subalgebras every
restriction multiplicity is 0 or 1 and the branching graph is
automatically a graph, never a multigraph.

This is forced for every P of every size, so a measurement of it cannot
do any work and is NOT booked as evidence here.  (mg-6ad0's X5 is the
finding that this repo has booked forced answers as MEASURED before.)
It is stated because it settles which side of T1/T2 we are on: we have
the hypothesis that does not carry the conclusion, and we fail the one
that does.

==========================================================================
T1a  dim TL_n = sum_p (dim V_{n,p})^2  --- the PATH-PAIR count
==========================================================================
A basis indexed by pairs of paths with a common endpoint exists iff
dim A = sum over the top-level vertices of (number of paths)^2.

  n dim TL_n    Catalan cell-module dims (dim V_{n,p})
  1        1          1  [1]   sum of squares = 1
  2        2          2  [1, 1]   sum of squares = 2
  3        5          5  [1, 2]   sum of squares = 5
  4       14         14  [1, 3, 2]   sum of squares = 14
  5       42         42  [1, 4, 5]   sum of squares = 42
  6      132        132  [1, 5, 9, 5]   sum of squares = 132

The cell-module dimensions do not depend on beta: the diagram basis and
the link states are defined without reference to it.  So the path-pair
count is the SAME at every parameter.

restriction multiplicity.

  V_{2,1} (dim 1) -> multiplicities over q=0.. : [1]

T1c  SEMISIMPLICITY, by two disjoint routes
==========================================================================
route 1: rad(A) = radical of the trace form of the regular
         representation (char 0), CHECKED to be a two-sided ideal and
         to be nilpotent.
route 2: Graham-Lehrer's criterion for a cellular algebra --- the
         semisimple quotient has dimension sum_p (rank of the Gram
         matrix of V_{n,p})^2.  Built from the bilinear form on link
         states; shares no code with route 1.

==========================================================================
T1d  THE VERDICT --- which hypothesis is load-bearing
==========================================================================
At n = 6 the branching graph is the same multiplicity-free graph for
every beta, and the path-pair count is 132 for every beta.  What
changes is only whether the algebra is an endomorphism algebra:

  beta   dim TL_6 dim of sum_lambda End(L_lambda) endomorphism algebra?
     3        132                        132            YES
     2        132                        132            YES
     1        132                         99             no
     0        132                         42             no

  Multiplicity-freeness is held FIXED across those four rows and the
  conclusion changes.  So multiplicity-freeness is not what carries it.
  Semisimplicity varies across those four rows exactly in step with the
  conclusion.

  What survives without semisimplicity is the BASIS, not the
  DECOMPOSITION: dim TL_n = sum_p (dim V_{n,p})^2 holds at every beta
  (T1a), so the pairs-of-paths basis exists throughout; the direct sum
  of endomorphism algebras does not.

    'At n = 6 the branching graph is the same multiplicity-free graph

instrument quotes; the path-pair count was, and equality of that one
statistic was taken for identity of the structure.  T1b2 measures the
branching graph itself, at every parameter.  It is not the same graph:
the vertex set differs at beta = 0 and multiplicities reach 2 at

  beta   dim TL_6 dim of sum_lambda End(L_lambda) endomorphism algebra?   branching mult-free?
     3        132                        132            YES                    YES
     2        132                        132            YES                    YES
     1        132                         99             no                     NO
     0        132                         42             no                     NO

  The last two columns move TOGETHER.  This tower does not separate the
  two hypotheses in either direction, so it cannot decide which of them
  carries the conclusion, and no claim that it does is made here.

  WHAT THE VERDICT NOW RESTS ON, and it is not a construction.  A
  finite direct sum sum_lambda End(V_lambda) IS semisimple, so for
  EVERY finite-dimensional algebra

      A  =  sum_lambda End(V_lambda)     <=>     A is semisimple,

  with no rank function, no branching graph and no multiplicity
  hypothesis anywhere in it.  That is Wedderburn in both directions and
  it settles the 'is' before any object is built.  Multiplicity-
  freeness buys the word 'canonically' and nothing else, by Vershik-
  Okounkov's Remark 1.3, quoted in T4.

  What survives without semisimplicity is the COUNT, not the

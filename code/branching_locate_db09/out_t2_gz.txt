==========================================================================
T2a  POSITIVE CONTROL --- the full symmetric group tower
==========================================================================
The number of paths from the root to level n in the branching graph is
sum_lambda f^lambda = the number of involutions in S_n.  Okounkov-
Vershik Prop. 1.1: GZ(n) is the algebra of operators diagonal in the
Gelfand-Tsetlin basis, hence has that dimension and is MAXIMAL
commutative.

  n  dim GZ(n)  # involutions    dim centralizer   maximal?
  1          1              1                  1        yes
  2          2              2                  2        yes
  3          4              4                  4        yes
  4         10             10                 10        yes
  5         26             26                 26        yes

==========================================================================
T2b  MULTIPLICITY-FREENESS SWITCHED OFF, SEMISIMPLICITY KEPT
==========================================================================
Same algebra C[S_4] --- still semisimple, still a sum of endomorphism
algebras --- but the chain has a level removed, so the branching is no
longer multiplicity-free.  Okounkov-Vershik Remark 1.3 predicts the GZ
algebra stops being maximal commutative, and that is what is measured.

chain                      dim GZ      # paths  dim centralizer     maximal?
S_1 < S_2 < S_3 < S_4          10           10               10          yes
S_1 < S_2 < S_4                 8           10               14           NO
S_1 < S_3 < S_4                 7           10               16           NO
S_1 < S_4                       5           10               24           NO
S_1 < S_2 < S_3 < S_4 < S_5         26           26               26          yes
S_1 < S_2 < S_3 < S_5          18           26               42           NO

  Every skipped chain has dim GZ strictly below the number of paths,
  and is not maximal commutative.  The GELFAND-TSETLIN BASIS is what
  multiplicity-freeness buys, and it is what is lost.

==========================================================================
T2d  THE CONCLUSION SURVIVES ANYWAY --- sum_lambda End(V_lambda) holds
==========================================================================
C[S_4] is semisimple whatever chain is drawn through it, so Wedderburn
still gives C[S_4] = sum_lambda End(V^lambda).  Measured: the radical
is zero, and the dimension is a sum of squares of the irreducible
dimensions 1,1,2,3,3.
  dim C[S_4] = 24, dim rad = 0, 1+1+4+9+9 = 24

  And the minimal witness, with no group in sight: C inside M_2(C).
  The unique simple of M_2 restricts to 2 copies of the simple of C, so
  the Bratteli diagram has a DOUBLE EDGE.
  dim M_2 = 4, dim rad = 0  ->  M_2 = End(V), V of dimension 2
  # paths from the root to the top vertex = 2
  dim GZ = 1  (< 2), dim centralizer of GZ = 4  -> NOT maximal commutative

==========================================================================
T2e  THE VERDICT
==========================================================================
Dropping multiplicity-freeness costs the CANONICAL BASIS and nothing
else: the algebra is still a direct sum of endomorphism algebras, and
paths still index a basis of each irreducible --- but the path basis is
no longer determined, because GZ is no longer maximal commutative and
the decomposition into irreducibles of the smaller algebra involves a
choice of splitting of a multiplicity space.

Dropping semisimplicity costs the CONCLUSION (T1).  The two hypotheses
are not on a par.

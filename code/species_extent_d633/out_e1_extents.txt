EXTENT OF THIS NUMBER.  Five checkers, each run ONCE, unmodified, under
an instrumented `open`.  It compares WHAT WAS READ with WHAT WAS
PRINTED and nothing else: it does not test whether a checker's rules
are right, whether its list is complete, or whether it FIRES on what it
reads.  A checker that opens every file and looks at none of them
passes E1 and fails E3, which is why both exist.

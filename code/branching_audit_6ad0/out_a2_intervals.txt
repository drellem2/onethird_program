A2a  THE SMALLEST WITNESS.  B2 says the posets P with J(P) an interval
     of Young's lattice are EXACTLY the cell posets.  Here is one that
     is not a cell poset.
==============================================================================

  P = the 2-element ANTICHAIN.
    J(P) has 4 elements: [[], [0], [1], [0, 1]]
    2-cell straight shapes: ['(2,)', '(1, 1)'] -- their posets are both the 2-CHAIN
    is P a cell poset D_lambda?  NO
    the Young-lattice interval [(1), (2,1)] = ['(1,)', '(2,)', '(1, 1)', '(2, 1)']   (4 elements)
    the skew cell poset (2,1)/(1) has cells [(0, 1), (1, 0)] and is isomorphic to
    the 2-element antichain: YES
    J((2,1)/(1)) is isomorphic to the interval as a poset: YES

  CONCLUSION A2a: B2's word "exactly" is FALSE.  The 2-element
  antichain is not a cell poset and J(antichain_2) IS an interval of
  Young's lattice.  The correct class is the SKEW cell posets.

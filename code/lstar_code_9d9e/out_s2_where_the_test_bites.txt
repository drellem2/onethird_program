s2.2  THE SHAPE-A CEILING, AND WHY 16,777,063 IS TWO STATEMENTS AND ONE NUMBER
==============================================================================
   The best shape-A constant ANY code can deliver over all P at a given n is
   log2 n! / (n log2 n) -- the free bound at the antichain, divided by n log2 n:

             n | log2 n!/(n log2 n)
     ----------+--------------------
            10 |            0.655976
           100 |            0.789850
          1000 |            0.855868
        100000 |            0.913147
      10000000 |            0.937958
      16777063 |            0.939888
     1000000000 |            0.951745
       It RISES to 1.  So a shape-A constant c < 1 valid at every P is refuted at the antichain
       for every n above 2^(log2 e / (1-c)) -- which is the predecessor's own crossover law
       read from the other end.

     the PRINTED constant 0.9399 gives n* = 16834249 -- a difference of 57186, which is

s2.4  WHY PASSING IS FREE WHERE IT CAN BE RUN — what hypothesis (1) buys, cumulatively
==============================================================================
   n = 5, all non-chain posets, bucketed by delta:

     delta | posets | max e(P) | CUMULATIVE max e over {delta <= this} | as % of free
    -------+--------+----------+--------------------------------------+--------------
       1/3 |    360 |        3 |                                    3 |  22.9%
      4/11 |    120 |       11 |                                   11 |  50.1%
       3/8 |    120 |        8 |                                   11 |  50.1%
       2/5 |    600 |       25 |                                   25 |  67.2%
       3/7 |    240 |        7 |                                   25 |  67.2%
       4/9 |    240 |        9 |                                   25 |  67.2%
      7/15 |    240 |       15 |                                   25 |  67.2%
       1/2 |   2191 |      120 |                                  120 | 100.0%
       THE COLUMN THAT MATTERS IS THE CUMULATIVE ONE, because a hypothesis is `delta <= t` and
       what it buys is the max over everything at or below t.  It runs 3 -> 120, i.e. 22.9% of
       the free bound at hypothesis (1)'s own threshold and 100% at 1/2.
       ⚠️ THE PER-BUCKET COLUMN IS **NOT** MONOTONE AND THAT IS PRINTED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED:
       delta = 2/5 carries e(P) = 25 while delta = 3/7 above it carries 7.  So `smaller delta
       means smaller e(P)` is FALSE bucket by bucket; what is true is the cumulative statement,
       and it is the cumulative statement a hypothesis consumes.
       Hypothesis (1) therefore buys exactly the regime where the free bound is already loose
       by an order -- which is why every code passes there, and why passing carries no
       information.  The test discriminates only in the regime where it is impossible to pass.

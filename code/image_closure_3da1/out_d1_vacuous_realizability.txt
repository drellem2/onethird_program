  [GREEN] hypothesis (1) read on the MEASURE excludes n! - 1 of the n! vertices
       THIS IS THE DIVIDING LINE, and it is the reason d1.2 is a finding rather than a
       tautology about every set anyone might write down.  A restriction tightens a linear
       ceiling exactly when it excludes vertices; realizability excludes none, and hypothesis
       (1) on the measure excludes all but one.
       AND IT IS ALSO WHY mg-c776 c2.3 IS NOT A COUNTEREXAMPLE TO THIS: c2.3 reads hypothesis
       (1) on the POSET -- delta(P) for P = P(pi) -- where a total order has no incomparable
       pair and its delta is a maximum over the EMPTY set, so all n! vertices survive.  The two
       readings of the same hypothesis differ by n! - 1 vertices, and d3 measures what the
       difference is worth.

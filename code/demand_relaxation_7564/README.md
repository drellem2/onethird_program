Deliverable: [`docs/OneThird-DemandRelaxation-mg-7564.md`](../../docs/OneThird-DemandRelaxation-mg-7564.md).

## What this instrument is for, and — first — what it is deliberately NOT for

`mg-7564`'s question is *"can the demand `ε_dem` be relaxed downstream instead of proved?"*
That is a **corpus search followed by an arithmetic join**, not a measurement. **This
directory enumerates no posets and measures nothing.** Every empirical input — `ε_leak`,
`C₃^gap`, `c`, `gap`, `min_k Q_k` — is **typed in from the document that measured it, with
its status attached**, and `lib7564.py` records the citation next to the constant.
Agreement with those documents is therefore *arithmetic reproduction*, never corroboration
of the underlying measurement.

Three things nevertheless had to be **run** rather than read:

1. **The four chains, re-solved from each chain's own `Φ` bound** rather than copied from
   `mg-76b2` §6 or `mg-9461` `s1` — on code sharing no line with `lib9461.py`, so a
   mis-transcription fails the plug-back check (`d0` §A, `d1` §A).
2. **THE JOIN NOTHING IN THE CORPUS PERFORMS.** `mg-6bc2` §3.1 states `1/150` in the
   `d·q̄` currency against `ε_spec = 1/50` and states **no other row**; `mg-9461` states the
   relaxed `ε_dem` values and never converts them. `d1` §C is the two-currency ladder, and
   `d1` §B is the grep that establishes it is new (with each raw hit **adjudicated by
   reading it**, not by tuning the pattern until it agreed with me).
3. **`mg-00b3`'s IN-REGIME `C₃^gap` carried back into the ladder** (`d2`). `mg-9461` priced
   the chain question using `n = 3..6` values, **every one of them out of regime**;
   `mg-00b3` measured the same constant inside the budget afterwards, and nothing has put
   the two together.

Everything on a decision path is `Fraction`. `lib7564._sqrt` **refuses an irrational root
rather than floating it** — and that refusal fired during development, which is why `d0`'s
perturbation factor is `(101/100)²` and not `(101/100)`; the reason is printed in the
transcript rather than left in a commit message.

## What the controls buy, and the one they cannot

`d0`'s negative arms are what make its passes results rather than tautologies — a
mis-derived chain (IV) that pays a Cheeger square **fails** plug-back; a `d·q̄` join with
the `n/(n+1)` factor dropped **breaks** the round trip at finite `n` and **passes** in the
limit, which is exactly the shape of error the `mg-832f` audit caught in `mg-6bc2` §3.1's
own table (it *"errs on the SAFE side"*, and this instrument prints the limit row and says
so).

**The control this instrument cannot have** is one on its inputs. If `mg-00b3`'s
`C₃^gap(S_25) = 10.1654` is wrong, every row in `d2` §A is wrong with it and nothing here
would notice. That is why `d2`'s header says every input is cited rather than measured, and
why the deliverable attributes each row to the document that owns it.

honest record is that this instrument's most useful output is its least pre-registered one.

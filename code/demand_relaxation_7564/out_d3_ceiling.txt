A. THE CEILING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   eps_leak                          = 1/5 (0.20)   EMPIRICAL, L4's threshold
   CEILING  eps_dem <= 2 * eps_leak  = 2/5 (0.40)
     in the d*qbar currency          <= 2/15 = 1 in 7.5
     as a density (qbar = 1/3)       <= 0.4000 (40.0% of pairs incomparable)
   RESIDUAL WALL AT THE CEILING      >= 2.50x (eps_sup = 1, PROVEN, an EQUALITY)

   Against the architecture as written (1/50), the ceiling is a relaxation
   of at most 20.00x — and NOT 50x, which is what closing
   the wall would need.  The gap does not close from the demand side, and this
   is now a PROOF rather than an exhausted enumeration.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. THE NON-VACUITY CAVEAT, STATED AND NOT BURIED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
   The argument shows Step 5's CONCLUSION is false at any poset with
   1 - lambda_std > 2*eps_leak = 0.40.  The DEMAND is an implication
   over the FROZEN class, so it could still hold vacuously at a larger
   eps_spec if the frozen class contains no poset in the window
   (0.40, eps_spec].  Two things to say about that, and they
   both point the same way:

   1. IF the frozen class IS empty in that window, then `frozen ==> 1-lambda_std
      <= 0.40` is TRUE — which is L1b itself, at eps_spec = 2/5.
      That is the wall proved, not the demand relaxed, and it is a strictly
      harder statement than anything on the demand ladder.  So this branch
      cannot be assumed; assuming it assumes the open lemma.
   2. IF it is NOT empty, the ceiling BINDS and eps_dem <= 2*eps_leak.

   EITHER WAY the demand side is capped at 2*eps_leak, and either way the
   residual wall is at least 2.50x.  The disjunction is the result;
   neither branch is a closure.

   ⚠️ AND THE CAP MOVES WITH eps_leak, WHICH ERRS OPTIMISTIC (mg-9461 §4.3).
      At mg-d3c7's required-scope n<=7 ceiling eps_leak <= 1/7 the cap is
      2/7 = 0.285714 and the wall is at least 3.50x; at the uniform value 0 the cap is 0.

========================================================================================================
S1.2  THE FLOOR — c#(P) >= Delta_P - gamma/2 AT EVERY POSET, EXACT, 0 EXCEPTIONS
========================================================================================================
The whole content of the floor is `gamma <= mu_pref`, because
    c# - (Delta - gamma/2) = [ sweep(mu,Delta) - 2 Delta gamma + gamma^2 ] / (2 gamma)
and with sweep(mu,Delta) = mu(2Delta-mu) the bracket is q(gamma) where
    q(t) = t^2 - 2 Delta t + mu(2Delta-mu) = (t - mu)(t - (2Delta - mu)) >= 0 for t <= mu,
while in the other branch sweep = Delta^2 and q(t) = (t-Delta)^2 >= 0 outright.
So the machine check is ONE EXACT DECISION PER POSET — `gamma > mu_pref` must be
FALSE — and it involves no bracket and no float.

  `gamma > mu_pref` at 0 of 4377 primitive posets n <= 6   (exact; 0 is the theorem)

  MUTATION CONTROL (PREDICTIONS.md E3b): the floor with the sign flipped,

========================================================================================================
S1.4  THE SWEEP'S LOSS, DECOMPOSED — WHAT EACH ROUTE THROWS AWAY
========================================================================================================
Pointwise, by definition,   c#(P) = Lam_M(P) * c_true(P)   and   f*(P) = Lam_F(P)^2 * c_true(P)
with  Lam_M = sweep(mu,Delta)/Phi*_pref^2   THE SWEEP'S LOSS (Cauchy-Schwarz + the
degree rounding + the cone penalty), and  Lam_F = M/Phi*_pref  THE MEDIANT LOSS (the
m-weighted MEAN of the prefix-conductance profile over its MINIMUM).

========================================================================================================
S1.7  WHY THE DISJUNCTION SURVIVES — THE TWO LOSSES ARE ANTI-CORRELATED IN gamma
========================================================================================================
This is the mechanism behind S1.6, and it is a statement about the SWEEP, not about
either route.  The sweep's loss has two parts that respond to gamma in OPPOSITE ways:

  * THE CONE PENALTY  mu_pref/gamma  — the price of insisting the test vector be
    monotone.  It is what makes c# exceed its floor, so it is the only part of (M#)
    a better vector could ever attack.
  * THE MEDIANT LOSS  M/Phi*_pref    — the m-weighted MEAN of the prefix-conductance
    profile over its MINIMUM.  It is the whole of (F)'s loss.

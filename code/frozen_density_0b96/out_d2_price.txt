m3  THE CROSSING, AND mg-33f5's T2
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    2/n <= eps_dem*(n+1)/n  <=>  2 <= eps_dem*(n+1)  <=>  n >= 2/eps_dem - 1
    first n at which a primitive poset can meet the needed ceiling:  n = 99

    mg-33f5 §3's T2 reads `2/eps_spec = 100'.  That drops the n/(n+1) factor between
    eps_sup and d; carrying it gives 99.  THE TWO ARE THE SAME THRESHOLD to one unit,
    and this arm reaches it from the density side rather than the master-bound side.
    Neither is corrected here: T2 is stated at its own precision and is right there.

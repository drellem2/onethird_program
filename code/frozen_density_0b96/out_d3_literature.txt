m4  WHY NOTHING BELOW n = 8, AND WHY AN INSTRUMENT STOPPING AT 7 WOULD HAVE LIED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
      first n with a non-empty residue: 8   OK

    Every poset on n <= 7 elements is 6-thin, because an element cannot be incomparable
    with more than n-1 <= 6 others.  So Peczarski's 6-thin exclusion covers the whole
    population below n = 8 ON ITS OWN, and `the literature decides everything' is what
    an exhaustive sweep to n = 7 would have reported -- a fact about the population's
    size, read as a fact about the literature's reach.
      max incomparability degree over every poset at n = 7:  6  (<= 6, as the argument
      says -- measured, not assumed)

    THE SURVEY VERDICT.  Of the 7 class exclusions the record carries, 5 deliver any
    upper bound on d at all at n = 9, the strongest of them 5/6 = 0.8333 -- and 0 deliver
    one anywhere near the 2e-2 row 8 needs (d2 m1).  Jointly they leave a residue whose
    density reaches 7/9 at n = 9 and, on the explicit family, 739/780 at n = 40.
    The literature bounds delta from BELOW and the exclusions cut the SPARSE side;
    cutting the sparse side of a class yields a LOWER bound on d, never an upper one,
    which is mg-345e's finding reproduced from the literature side instead of a grep.

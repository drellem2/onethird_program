  discriminating', and section 2.2 of the document says 'the two that

  the opposite algebra of convention A, in which case iso/B and anti/A
  are THE SAME STATEMENT and there are two hypotheses, not four.

  Convention B IS the opposite algebra of convention A, identically.
  So the four columns of T3d are two statements, each computed twice:
      {iso/A, anti/B} is one statement -- and it fails;
      {anti/A, iso/B} is the other  -- and it holds.
  The theorem is reproduced and the comparison IS discriminating; what
  is not the case is that it survived three independent controls.  It
  survived ONE, run twice.  mg-7d75's section 2.2 says 'three of the
  four columns are the control, and they fire' -- two of those three
  columns are the same control in a mirror, and one of them is not a
  control at all: iso/B is the surviving identification.

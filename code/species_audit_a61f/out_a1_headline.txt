A1a  the identity on all 87 classes to n <= 5, NO CAP, via the trace form
==============================================================================

  dim A/rad is computed as the RANK OF THE TRACE FORM B(x,y) = tr(L_xy)
  (Dickson: in characteristic 0 the Jacobson radical is the radical of
  that form).  Rank is taken modulo two primes, 2147483647 and 1000003; a
  disagreement between them would be reported as a failure.

==============================================================================
A1d  THE PROOF, and an exact check of its two steps
==============================================================================

  Write A = kF(P), G = Aut(P), k of characteristic 0.

  (1)  A/rad A = k^{AC(P)}.  This is Bidigare's radical theorem, which
       mg-7d75 quotes in full from Aguiar-Mahajan section 10.10 and
       which Brown extended to left regular bands: rad A = ker(supp).

  (2)  G acts on A by algebra automorphisms (it permutes F(P) and the
       Tits product is equivariant), and |G| is invertible in k, so the
       Reynolds operator e = (1/|G|) sum_g g is a projection A -> A^G
       and taking G-invariants is EXACT.  Applying it to
       0 -> rad A -> A -> k^{AC} -> 0 gives
            A^G / (rad A)^G  =  ( k^{AC} )^G  =  k^{AC/G}.

  (3)  (rad A)^G is a nilpotent ideal of A^G, so it lies in rad(A^G);
       and the quotient above is a product of copies of k, hence
       semisimple, so rad(A^G) lies in (rad A)^G.  They are equal, and
            A^G / rad(A^G)  =  k^{ AC(P)/G }.

  Three lines, no new object, and no dependence on n.  What follows for
  the audit is in the ledger, not here: the identity is a COROLLARY of
  the theorem mg-7d75 quotes, not a measurement awaiting a citation.

  Step (2) and step (3) checked EXACTLY over Q, all classes to n <= 4:

==============================================================================
A1e  CONTROLS -- two WRONG index sets, one of them this repo's own error
==============================================================================

  If dim A^G/rad were insensitive to the index set, A1a would prove
  nothing.  Two substitutes are run against the same left-hand side.

  (i)  Pi[n]/G in place of AC(P)/G  -- the full partition lattice.
  (ii) the OPEN-cone flats in place of AC(P): the partitions in AC(P)
       whose every block is an antichain of P.  Conflating these two is
       exactly the error mg-1953 repaired (R1, 455 spurious flats at
       n = 6), and code/species_7d75/README.md names it as the
       convention that has bitten this repo before.

   n  classes  (i) disagrees  (i) agrees  (ii) disagrees  (ii) agrees
   2        2              0            2               1            1
   3        5              1            4               4            1
   4       16             10            6              15            1
   5       63             55            8              62            1

  Both controls fire.  The right-hand side of the identity is not a
  free parameter: AC(P)/Aut(P) is the index set and neither of the two
  nearby candidates reproduces the dimension.

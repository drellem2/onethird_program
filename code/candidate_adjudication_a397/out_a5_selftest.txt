§4  WHAT THIS DIRECTORY DOES NOT ESTABLISH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    (1) a4 measures the check's answer on the HEALTHY world only.  FALSE-ON-GOOD
        says the check is not already satisfied; it does NOT say the check would
        fire.  Only a2 runs its subject both ways, and only over 19 sites.
    (2) POLARITY is read off the needle by a regex over words like FAIL and
        Traceback.  A negative-control expectation whose marker is an ordinary
        English phrase is read as POSITIVE and lands in CANNOT-TELL.  That is a
        conservative error and it is the reason CANNOT-TELL is large.
    (3) THE REGISTRY'S SUBJECT SENTENCES ARE NOT TOUCHED, for the reason c9876
        gives: a probe is written FROM the subject, so a subject that misdescribes
        its arm yields a probe that agrees with it.  Nothing here claims to
        settle that and nothing here should be read as having tried.

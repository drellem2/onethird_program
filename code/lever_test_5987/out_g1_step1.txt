§4. SO THE READING IS PRICED RATHER THAN PICKED.  This directory evaluates both statements at the
    BARYCENTRIC reference — sort by `h(x) = E[pos_σ x]`, always a linear extension because `x < y`
    forces `h(x) < h(y)`.  Two measurements justify it, and the first is the one that makes every
    later verdict reading-proof.

   (a) the ENVELOPE over ALL linear extensions as reference, n ≤ 6, primitives.  Every
       number is a FLOOR over the class — the smallest value the primitives take under that
       reading — because the floor is what step 2 prices.  ⚠️  THE FAVOURABLE END IS NOT THE
       SAME END FOR THE TWO OBJECTS, which is measured here rather than assumed: a LOWER
       floor delivers FEWER orders, so `min over e` is the reading most favourable to the
       lever, and the barycentric order sits at that end for (EQ) and at the OTHER end for
       (B-cov) — it roughly minimises inversions, and inversions are (B-cov)'s DENOMINATOR.
             (EQ): max_x |h − rank_e|                     (B-cov): Σ C_x / E[inv_e]
        min over e | max over e | bary at min |  min over e | max over e | bary at max

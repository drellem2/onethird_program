D0g  NOTHING THIS TICKET PRINTS INTO ANOTHER TREE IS A COUNT ROW
==========================================================================

  This ticket adds three kinds of line to two other trees' published
  transcripts: a dated `population:` line, a CLASS line, and an
  OBSERVED interval block.  If any of them parsed as a COUNT ROW it
  would enter the arc's own census -- a ticket about dating the census
  would have changed it, in every tree that measures it, silently.
  So the lines are generated HERE and put to `lib56dc.count_rows`:

      |  population: one dated at a UNION of two refs   @9f1ecaa+eacc5e1

      |  else: is the population a REF, and is the observer IN it.

      |  OBSERVED, the two readings the apparatus admits -- width is one
      |  call to `libfd9c.weight_of`, this tree's own transcripts read as
      |  EMPTY as a plain `>` leaves them.  NOT AN ERROR BAR.
      |      files    854-854 @518a5e9 (OBSERVED)     <- EMPTY INTERVAL
      |      rows     2176-2233 @518a5e9 (OBSERVED)
      |      erows    572-576 @518a5e9 (OBSERVED)

      ^ one unit of that number is one printed line

  THE ARM IS NOT `I LOOKED AND THEY SEEMED FINE`.  `_COUNT_ROW` wants

  The first check is the one that matters and it points the unusual
  way: a site FAILS if its figure moved.  Nothing was recomputed here,
  and this is how a reader confirms that without diffing four files.

  population: the 10 PROSE SITES above   @518a5e9

      ^ one unit of that number is one line of a tracked `.md`
      ...of them whose FIGURE CHANGED -- must be 0                    0
      ^ one unit of that number is one line of a tracked `.md`
      ...of them that VANISHED -- must be 0                           0
      ^ one unit of that number is one line of a tracked `.md`
      ...of them now carrying a REF beside the figure                10
      ^ one unit of that number is one line of a tracked `.md`

      Every site kept its published digits and gained a class and a
      ref.  `517` still says 517; what it now also says is that
      517 was a reading of `9f1ecaa + eacc5e1` and is OBSERVED --
      so a reader who re-runs the probe and gets 849 has measured
      the arc's growth rather than found a refutation.

  AND THE MARKER IS EXPLAINED IN EACH FILE, not in a commit message.
  Each of the four carries the same closing note: the three classes,
  what `@9f1ecaa+eacc5e1` means and why it is a union of two refs, and
  a sentence saying the figures above are NOT refreshed and must not
  be.  The note names no HEAD value -- putting today's number into
  prose is how this arc got the problem in the first place, and a
  ticket about dating figures that added a fresh undated one would be
  its own counterexample.

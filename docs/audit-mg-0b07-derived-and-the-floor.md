## 4. The floor — where it actually is

`p3`. The subject's claim is read out of the tree here and it is **exact**: `absorb_trace`
contains **0 boolean operators of any kind**, not merely 0 "deciding" ones. No enumerated
clause contains a `BoolOp` anywhere below it. Its enumerated population is 4 of the file's 16
boolean operators, which is what its sentence says.

**And that is not the question.** The regress is about the difference between the unit a
test perturbs and the unit its result is read at. A list comparison is a disjunction:

    [w for w in A] != [w for w in B]   <=>   len(A) != len(B)  or  a common row width differs

| perturbation of the ONE-clause condition | artifact | exit | bytes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** — the ORDER half alone (`len(A) != len(B)`) | CHANGES | 1 | 24,909 |
| **S2** — the WIDTH half alone | **BYTE-IDENTICAL** | **0** | 23,695 |
| **S3** — the whole condition replaced by `False` | CHANGES | 1 | 24,909 |

3 of 3 predictions matched. And at `b6bc2ef`, where the same two meanings **are** the two
clauses mg-c4c8 enumerated, the same two answers come back — **CHANGES/1 and
BYTE-IDENTICAL/0 on both trees**. The units are the same units. S1 and S3 produce the *same*
artifact, so on this population the order half contributes nothing the width half does not.

## Findings

### B1 — THE THIRD RUNG WAS RESPELLED, NOT REMOVED

On the live tree the `shape` condition's **order half can be taken out with the width half
left standing and the artifact comes back byte-identical at 23,695 bytes, exit 0, every row
green.** That is mg-e7bc's sentence with `return` → `clause` → `sub-condition`, on the tree
this commit shipped.

The subject's claim — "no boolean operator, so the smallest deletable unit inside this gate
IS the `return`" — is **true**, and is about **deletion**. What changed is that the inert
half stopped being *nameable*, not that it stopped being *inert*. `absorb_trace`'s own
docstring says the two clauses "were saying one thing"; they were saying two, and one of
them is still invisible to the battery.

This is **not** a correctness finding: the subject's 28,900-pair equivalence holds and is
confirmed from the battery's side here — the rewrite preserved the behaviour. It preserved
the inertness too.

**To close it:** run S1/S2 beside `AFTER-5` as a two-row sub-condition sweep, or state on
`AFTER-5`'s line that the guard's condition still separates two sub-conditions and which of
them the battery can see.

### B2 — The `FINEST UNIT` line states the finest unit of the PATCH, and is read as the finest unit of the SITE

`AFTER-5` prints `FINEST UNIT THIS LINE PERTURBS: one \`return\` statement, and nothing
finer is removed`. Nothing finer is removed *by that patch*. mg-c4c8's OPEN 1 asked for the
finest unit to be stated beside the test **so a reader need not assume the evidence reaches
the bottom** — and at this site the two quantities differ, which is exactly the case the
sentence was added for.

**To close it:** one clause on that line, derived the way the rest of it is.

### B3 — Branch 7's stated reason is TRUE and its conclusion does not follow

The subject's self-defect enumeration names this branch — "the regress could continue below
a clause" — and answers it: *"CANNOT ARISE FOR THE DELETION TEST … deleting an operand of a
comparison does not leave a condition."* **The reason is checked here and it holds**: the
condition is an `ast.Compare` with one comparator, so neither operand can be removed with a
condition behind it.

**The conclusion drawn from it is refuted by B1.** The reason answers *can anything smaller
be DELETED*; the question this lineage is about is *can anything smaller be PERTURBED and go
unseen*, and the two come apart at exactly this site.

This is scored deliberately as **not a gap**. The branch was named, a reason was given, and
the reason is true — which is the outcome the brief asks to be treated as legitimate and
checked. It is the most useful row in the subject's list, because it is the one place where
the discipline was applied and the answer is still wrong.

### B4 — The grain-free channel has a grain, and it is the size

Branch 1 says the derived declaration has a grain of its own — `return`, statement and clause
are three *chosen* units — and closes it with `nodes`, "the count with no grain". `nodes` is
a **net difference of two totals**. Run through the subject's own `d2`:

| mutation put through `AFTER-4`'s slot | declaration | the "not coarser" claim |
|---|---|---|
| `abs(…)` dropped from **both** sides of the magnitude comparison | `0/0/0`, **6 nodes** | **[BROKEN]** — the check fires |
| `abs(…)` dropped on the left and **doubled on the right** | `0/0/0`, **0 nodes** | **[HOLDS]** — syntax left, nothing said |

The second is size-preserving and changes what the predicate tests. The remedy for "the
declaration has a grain" has the defect the branch describes, one rung further down.

**Scale:** the first row shows the check works wherever the patch is a net removal, which is
every mutation the instrument currently carries. This is a hole in the guard, not a wrong
number in the transcript.

**To close it:** compare the two trees' node **multisets**, or report removed and added
separately, so a substitution cannot present as a no-op.

### A1 — The subject's central transcript does not regenerate from the tree it is committed in

`out_d2_deletion.txt` differs from a re-run at **1 of 377 lines**, and the line is a count of
commits: *"of the 6 commits that ever touched face_complex.py"* where the committed tree has
**7** — because the commit publishing the transcript is itself the seventh. `out_d1_trace.txt`,
`out_d3_reintroduction.txt` and `out_d4_auditor_rerun.txt` all reproduce byte-identically.

The claim still HOLDS and `d2` still exits 0, so nothing is hidden. What is wrong is the
provenance, and this is the shape mg-821e's `b534db7` published a post-commit measurement to
fix in a sibling instrument. **It cannot be fixed by regenerating once:** the number counts
commits touching `face_complex.py`, so it moves again on the next one.

**To close it:** report the pin's identity without a history-dependent total — "the newest
two-clause commit is X and the pin resolves to X" is the claim; the count of all commits ever
touching the file is a number that no future tree can reproduce.

**And this audit inherits the drift by quoting it.** `out_p1_derived.txt` prints the two
differing lines verbatim, so the next commit that touches `face_complex.py` will make *this*
transcript stop regenerating too. That is said here rather than engineered around: the point
of the finding is that a history-dependent count cannot be pinned by regenerating, and an
audit that quoted it and then hid the consequence would be making the subject's argument for
it.

### A2 — The verdict column asserts a direction it never measures

The subject's column is `"exact" if got == reading else "*** UNDERSTATES ***"`. That
expression was lifted out of its source and **run here**: it prints `*** UNDERSTATES ***` for
a declaration that **overstates** its patch as well as for one that understates it, and the
claim beside the table reads "UNDERSTATE THEIR OWN PATCHES ON 8 OF 11" from a set built by
`r[1] != r[2]`.

It is right about all eight — re-measured independently in §2 — so the figure stands. The
direction is the whole content of the word: a declaration that **overstates** makes the
evidence look coarser, which is harmless; one that understates makes it look finer, which is
what mg-c4c8 found. A column that cannot tell them apart cannot report the next one.

### B5 — The finer grain is enumerated and the coarser one is still a hand-written table

mg-c4c8's F3 is disclosed as not closed and is confirmed unchanged here: `gate_violations`'s
and `diagonal_moves`'s shape guards are each a `return` whose individual deletion moves not
one byte. The shape worth recording is that **the clause sweep this commit added runs over an
ENUMERATED population while the return-level deletion test is still eleven hand-written
rows** — so a `return` added to the predicate layer tomorrow gets no deletion test and
nothing goes red, while a **clause** added does. The enumeration went to the rung the ticket
named and not to the one above it.

**To close it:** mg-c4c8's own wording — sweep the enumerated returns of the predicate layer.
